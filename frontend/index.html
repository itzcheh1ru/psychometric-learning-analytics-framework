GenAI-Assisted Cognitive Engagement, Recall, and Learning Retention Evaluation

A comparative empirical study investigating cognitive load, conceptual synthesis, and delayed knowledge retention across human-only and GenAI-assisted learning environments.

ⓘ
Research Data Collection Portal: This system is an empirical data collection prototype developed strictly for academic investigation. All participant responses and interaction telemetry are anonymized and stored solely for research evaluation.

Research Overview & Study Objective

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is reshaping how learners discover, synthesize, and interact with complex knowledge domains. While GenAI provides immediate answers and personalized summaries, its cognitive trade-offs—specifically concerning active recall, cognitive depth, and long-term retention—require rigorous empirical assessment.

This experiment evaluates participant performance across three systematic conditions to measure whether AI-assisted learning enhances or diminishes deep conceptual recall compared to autonomous cognitive engagement.

⏱ Total Estimated Duration: ~35–40 Minutes 🎓 Fixed-Order Within-Subjects Design 🔒 100% Anonymous & Confidential
📝

Before You Begin

Please review the following essential instructions before beginning your experimental session:

  • Complete experiment in one sitting: The study evaluates uninterrupted cognitive processing; please allocate a quiet, continuous session.
  • Follow the required order: Complete Brain-Only, GenAI-Assisted, and Delayed Recall in sequence.
  • Use GenAI only during permitted phase: Generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) are allowed only in the designated AI-assisted condition. External tools are strictly prohibited during independent and recall assessments.
  • Estimated duration: 35–40 minutes: Covers reading passages, immediate comprehension checks, and the final delayed retention test.

Experimental Workflow & Roadmap

All participants progress through the same standardized phases in order:

Condition 01 ⏱ ~15 Mins

Brain-only Learning

Participants review study material relying strictly on self-directed reading and autonomous cognitive synthesis without AI assistance, followed by an immediate comprehension assessment.

Assistance: None (Human-only)
Format: Study Passage + 10 MCQs + 2 Short Answers
Condition 02 ⏱ ~15 Mins

GenAI-Assisted Learning

Participants engage with advanced conceptual material while permitted and encouraged to use Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot), followed by comprehension and cognitive load checks.

Assistance: GenAI Tools Permitted
Format: Study Passage + 10 MCQs + 2 Short Answers
Condition 03 ⏱ ~10 Mins

Delayed Recall Test

A cumulative objective evaluation assessing memory consolidation, conceptual synthesis, and long-term retention across both conditions without external aids.

Assistance: No External Aids
Format: Dual-Section Retention Exam (24 items)
