Analytical Independence

Integration Principle: Complementary Evidence

Each analytical component produces independent findings from its own data source and methodology. These outputs are interpreted together to build a holistic understanding of GenAI-assisted learning. The framework does not combine component outputs into a single predictive model, overall risk score, or combined participant metric. No cross-component classification rules exist (e.g. "High Risk + Low Trust = Critical").

  • Each component operates on its own independent data instrument.
  • Analytical methods are methodologically distinct and cannot be trivially combined.
  • Component development is assigned to separate team members.
  • A delay in one component does not block progress in any other.
  • No overall AI risk score is computed or will be computed by this framework.
  • single_combined_model = false — confirmed in GET /api/framework/specification.
