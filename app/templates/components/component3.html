Study Pattern Shift

Average change in study-hour allocation compared with each participant’s previous available study week.

Study pattern shift is not yet available. At least two study weeks for the same participant are required.

Shift = the average of the absolute percentage-point changes in AI-assisted and independent study shares. One percentage point indicates a change in the share of reported study hours; this descriptive indicator is not a validated psychometric scale. Week 1 is a baseline and is not plotted as zero shift.
