Component 04

Cognitive Engagement & Learning Retention

Evaluates the impact of GenAI-assisted learning on cognitive engagement and knowledge retention through NLP-based writing analysis, recall evaluation, ownership measurement and paired statistical comparison of learning conditions.

Experimental Preparation / Data Collection

Methodology

NLP Writing Analysis

Lexical diversity, readability and syntactic complexity of student writing samples.

TF-IDF / Semantic Similarity

Measures content similarity between AI-assisted and brain-only writing conditions.

Recall & Retention Evaluation

Immediate and delayed recall assessment post-writing experiment.

Paired Statistical Testing

Within-participant statistical comparison of learning conditions.

Planned Analytical Outputs

  • Writing quality comparison (AI-assisted vs. brain-only)
  • Immediate recall scores
  • Delayed recall scores
  • Knowledge retention rate
  • Cognitive ownership measurement
  • Cognitive engagement indicators
  • Statistical comparison between learning conditions
