Experimental Research Design

Controlled laboratory and classroom evaluation contrasting independent cognitive effort against AI-assisted task execution.

CONDITION A

Brain-Only Writing

Student completes the assigned academic writing task without direct Generative AI assistance.

VS
CONDITION B

GenAI-Assisted Writing

Student completes the assigned academic writing task with permitted Generative AI assistance according to the study protocol.

CONTROLLED COMPARISON
Writing Output + Immediate Recall + Delayed Recall + Ownership + Cognitive Effort + Human Rubric + NLP Features
Methodological Principle: Better writing output does not automatically imply better learning or retention; output quality and learning evidence are evaluated separately.

Counterbalancing Protocol

Mitigating sequence and topic order effects across experimental conditions.

Counterbalanced condition order
Sequence A
Brain-Only ↓ GenAI-Assisted
Sequence B
GenAI-Assisted ↓ Brain-Only

When the same participants complete both experimental conditions, condition order and equivalent task topics should be controlled to reduce order and topic effects.
