About This Psychometric Self-Assessment

This diagnostic tool is part of our research study on Generative AI usage in undergraduate higher education. It presents 26 validated empirical survey items across five core behavioural dimensions. Upon completion, you will receive:

  • Continuous Construct Scores (1.00–5.00): Evaluated via unweighted indicator means and factor-score sensitivity checks.
  • Norm-Referenced Cohort Comparisons: Your percentile rank and quartile position relative to 375 undergraduate students.
  • Explainable Behavioural Profile: An evidence-based interpretation grounded in our Confirmatory Factor Analysis and Structural Equation Modelling findings.
  • Traceable Recommendations: Concrete, actionable study strategies directly linked to your specific questionnaire ratings.
Privacy & In-Memory Analytics: This assessment runs in-memory. Your individual responses and scores are evaluated directly in this browser session and are never stored in a permanent database or linked to your personal student identity without explicit research consent.

Scientific & Methodological Disclaimers:
  • Self-Report Epistemology: Responses represent your self-reported perceptions and study habits on a 5-point Likert scale, not direct instrumented telemetry or objective task performance.
  • Continuous Dimensions: Behavioural profiles and quadrant boundaries (AT median = 3.33, VB median = 3.80) are descriptive conventions derived from our empirical sample (N=375). They do not represent clinical cutoffs or diagnostic risk classifications.
  • Structural Associations: SEM path estimates demonstrate cross-sectional covariance relationships across the student cohort and do not establish deterministic causal effects for an individual learner.
