Assessment Protocol & Scientific Methodology Notice

Purpose of this Practical Assessment

While the 26-item student questionnaire measures self-reported verification habits, this practical assessment measures your demonstrated evaluative accuracy when reviewing simulated AI-generated academic outputs.

Controlled Error Topology: The scenarios contain an authentic distribution of correct academic claims, subtle numerical and calculation errors, conceptual logical fallacies, and phantom academic citations, alongside fully accurate scientific controls.

Instrument Status: Developed as an educational research assessment engine (Project J26-DS-310). Claims are scored against expert reference solutions (Stewart Calculus, Casella & Berger, Atkins, Tindall & Kunkel, Vaswani et al.).

Empirical Validation Roadmap & Scientific Disclaimers:
  • Researcher-Designed Task Status: The 8 controlled scenarios and rubric criteria were constructed by educational measurement researchers for empirical validation (Project J26-DS-310). Content validity indexing (I-CVI ≥ 0.78) with an expert panel is underway.
  • Self-Report vs. Performance Distinction: Survey responses reflect self-reported attitudes and perceived habits, whereas task evaluations measure demonstrated accuracy on specific problem instances. Discrepancy indices (Δ = VBnorm − Accuracy) serve exploratory pedagogical purposes and do not represent a clinical metacognitive diagnostic.
  • Baseline Data Preservation: The original N=375 empirical survey dataset is preserved untouched for baseline psychometric reference and is never retrofitted with task performance data.
