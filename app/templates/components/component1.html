Research Instrument & Study Context

Undergraduate Learning Analytics

Generative AI tools can support academic study when used for conceptual exploration and structured feedback. However, substituting individual analytical struggle with automated outputs—termed cognitive offloading—can attenuate critical reasoning and problem-solving retention. This instrument collects 20 behavioural indicators to quantify individual offloading risk on a 1.000–5.000 latent scale across four empirical tertiles: Low (≤ 2.667), Moderate (2.668–3.500), and High (> 3.500).
