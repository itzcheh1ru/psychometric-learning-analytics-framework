Component 02

Algorithmic Trust & Verification Analysis

Analyses the psychological relationships between algorithmic trust, perceived usefulness, verification behaviour, AI dependence and learning confidence using Confirmatory Factor Analysis and Structural Equation Modelling.

Data Collection / Module Preparation

Psychometric Constructs

Algorithmic Trust

Degree to which students trust GenAI-generated outputs.

Perceived Usefulness

Perceived benefit of GenAI for academic tasks.

Verification Behaviour

Frequency and depth of checking AI outputs independently.

AI Dependence

Extent to which learning relies on GenAI assistance.

Learning Confidence

Self-assessed confidence in independent understanding.

Methodology

Confirmatory Factor Analysis

Validates measurement model reliability and construct validity.

Structural Equation Modelling

Models causal pathways between latent constructs using R/lavaan.

Reliability & Validity Assessment

Cronbach's alpha, composite reliability, AVE, discriminant validity.

Bootstrapping / Mediation

Indirect effects and mediation significance testing.

Planned Analytical Outputs

  • CFA measurement model with factor loadings
  • Model-fit indices (CFI, TLI, RMSEA, SRMR)
  • SEM pathway diagram with standardised coefficients
  • Direct and indirect effect estimates
  • Verification mediation analysis

Analytics

SEM analytics not yet available
