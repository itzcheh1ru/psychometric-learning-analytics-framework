Architecture Summary

The framework is structured around four fully independent analytical components. Each component is self-contained within its own route blueprint and service module, and operates on distinct data sources and methodologies.

This independence ensures that research conducted on one component does not affect or depend on another, allowing parallel development by each team member.

Once research data collection is complete and each component has been individually analysed, their outputs will be presented together in this shared research analytics dashboard for complementary interpretation. The four components are not combined into a single unified model.

No final research findings, model outputs, SEM coefficients, or statistical results are presented in this prototype. All analytical content will reflect actual research results after data collection is complete.
