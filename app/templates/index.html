Research Prototype

Psychometric Learning Analytics Framework

Project ID: J26-DS-310

A data-driven research framework designed to analyse the impact of Generative AI on undergraduate learning behaviour, cognitive engagement, algorithmic trust, and knowledge retention.

The framework integrates Explainable Machine Learning, Structural Equation Modelling (SEM), Longitudinal Learning Analytics, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to generate evidence-based insights for responsible AI adoption in higher education.

View Research Dashboard

Research Components

Four independent analytical modules that will be integrated into a unified research analytics dashboard upon data collection completion.

01

Cognitive Offloading Risk Prediction

Logistic Regression · Random Forest · XGBoost · SHAP

Predicts cognitive offloading risk based on students' GenAI usage, verification practices and learning strategies.

02

Algorithmic Trust & Verification

CFA · Structural Equation Modelling · R / lavaan

Models psychological pathways between trust, perceived usefulness, verification behaviour and AI dependence.

03

Longitudinal Study Pattern Analytics

Pandas · Time-Series Analysis · Trend Modelling

Tracks how AI-assisted and independent study patterns evolve over time across the research cohort.

04

Cognitive Engagement & Learning Retention

NLP · NLTK · spaCy · Statistical Analysis

Evaluates writing quality, lexical diversity and recall under AI-assisted and brain-only writing conditions.
