Research Prototype — J26-DS-310

Psychometric Learning Analytics Framework

A data-driven research framework analysing the impact of Generative AI on undergraduate learning behaviour, cognitive engagement, algorithmic trust, and knowledge retention across four independent analytical research components.

View Research Dashboard Project Information

Four Independent Research Components

Each component applies distinct analytical methodologies and remains methodologically independent. Their outputs will be integrated into a shared research analytics dashboard.

01

Cognitive Offloading Risk Prediction

ML · XGBoost · SHAP

Predicts cognitive-offloading risk using behavioural predictors with SHAP-based explainability.

02

Algorithmic Trust & Verification

CFA · SEM · Psychometrics

Models trust pathways between AI trust, verification behaviour and learning confidence using SEM.

03

Longitudinal Study Pattern Analytics

Longitudinal · Trend · Prompt Analysis

Tracks how AI-assisted and independent study behaviours evolve across the academic term.

04

Engagement & Learning Retention

NLP · Statistics · Recall

Evaluates writing quality, recall, retention and cognitive ownership using NLP and statistical testing.
