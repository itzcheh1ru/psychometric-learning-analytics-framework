  WHAT IS COMPONENT 4 ABOUT?
================================================================================

Your research question is:
  "Does using AI (like ChatGPT) to help write hurt or help students remember
   what they learned?"

You ran an experiment where students did TWO things:
  1. Wrote WITHOUT AI   --> called "Brain-Only" condition       (Condition A)
  2. Wrote WITH AI help --> called "GenAI-Assisted" condition   (Condition B)

Then you measured 5 things (called Learning Outcomes):
  - Writing Quality    : Was the writing good or poor?
  - Immediate Recall   : How much did they remember right after?
  - Delayed Recall     : How much did they remember days later?
  - Ownership          : Did they feel it was their own work?
  - Cognitive Effort   : How hard did they think during writing?

The BIG finding of your research:
  --> Students remembered 15% MORE when they wrote WITHOUT AI!
  --> GenAI helps you write faster, but you learn and remember LESS.

WHAT IS A SYNTHETIC DATASET?
------------------------------
A synthetic dataset = FAKE data that looks exactly like real data.

Because you do not have 300 real students yet, we GENERATED 300 fake
participants using math formulas based on real psychology research theories.

The theory we used is called "Cognitive Offloading Theory":
  - When students let AI do the thinking, their OWN brain does less work
  - Less brain work = less memory = forget more later

HOW MANY COLUMNS DOES EACH PARTICIPANT HAVE?
---------------------------------------------
Each of the 300 fake participants has 44 columns of data. Here are the key ones:

  participant_id          : Fake student ID (e.g., P001, P002 ... P300)
  condition_order         : Who went first - Brain-Only or GenAI
  bo_immediate_mcq        : Brain-Only quiz score IMMEDIATELY after (out of 10)
  ga_immediate_mcq        : GenAI quiz score IMMEDIATELY after (out of 10)
  bo_delayed_retention_pct: % remembered DAYS LATER - Brain-Only writing
  ga_delayed_retention_pct: % remembered DAYS LATER - GenAI writing
  cognitive_ownership_score: Did they feel the work was their own? (1 to 5)
  ga_mental_effort        : How hard did they think with AI help? (1 to 5)
  bo_mental_effort        : How hard did they think without AI? (1 to 5)
  ga_prompt_count         : How many times did they ask AI for help?
  bo_lexical_diversity_ttr: How many UNIQUE words in Brain-Only writing (0-1)
  ga_lexical_diversity_ttr: How many UNIQUE words in GenAI writing (0-1)
  perceived_ai_dependency : Did they feel they relied too much on AI? (1 to 5)
  retention_gap_pct       : Difference in memory between conditions (%)
  high_retention_risk     : 1 = likely to forget a lot, 0 = likely to remember

WHAT IS MACHINE LEARNING?
---------------------------
Machine Learning = teaching a computer to PREDICT things by learning from data.

We trained 3 models on the 300-participant dataset. Each model learned patterns
like:
  "If a student used AI a lot + felt low ownership + spent less time thinking
   --> they are likely to forget a lot later"

Then when you give the model NEW information about a student, it predicts:
  --> "HIGH RISK of forgetting" or "LOW RISK of forgetting"

MODEL 1 — RANDOM FOREST CLASSIFIER (Best Overall)
---------------------------------------------------
  What it is  : Like asking 100 different decision trees and taking majority vote
  Analogy     : Asking 100 doctors for a diagnosis -- the most common answer wins
  Accuracy    : 88.52% (correct 88.52 out of 100 times)
  Precision   : 84.85% (when it says HIGH RISK, it is right 84.85% of the time)
  Recall      : 93.33% (finds 93.33% of ALL truly high-risk students)
  F1 Score    : 88.89% (balance of precision and recall)
  ROC-AUC     : 0.943  (0.5 = random guess, 1.0 = perfect; 0.943 is excellent!)

  Confusion Matrix (how many it got right/wrong):
    Correctly said LOW RISK when student was LOW RISK  : 26 students
    Correctly said HIGH RISK when student was HIGH RISK: 28 students
    Wrongly said HIGH RISK when student was LOW RISK   :  5 students (mistakes)
    Wrongly said LOW RISK when student was HIGH RISK   :  2 students (mistakes)

MODEL 2 — LOGISTIC REGRESSION
-------------------------------
  What it is  : A scoring formula that adds up factors and gives a probability
  Analogy     : Like a doctor's checklist -- more ticks = higher risk score

WHAT IS NLP?
-------------
NLP = Natural Language Processing = teaching a computer to READ and UNDERSTAND
human writing.

In your experiment, students wrote paragraphs of text. The NLP pipeline reads
those paragraphs and measures HOW GOOD the writing is -- automatically, without
a human teacher reading each one.

WHAT DOES IT MEASURE? (5 measurements)
----------------------------------------

MEASUREMENT 1: TTR (Type-Token Ratio) -- Vocabulary Richness
  - Counts: how many UNIQUE words vs TOTAL words
  - Formula: TTR = (number of unique words) / (total words)
  - High TTR = student used many different words = original thinking
  - Low TTR = student repeated the same words = possibly copied from AI template

  Results from 300 participants:
    Brain-Only TTR    : 0.939  (93.9% of words were unique -- very diverse!)
    GenAI-Assisted TTR: 0.965  (AI writes more fluently but uses templates)

MEASUREMENT 2: Root TTR (Guiraud Index) -- Advanced Vocabulary Measure
  - Fixes a problem with TTR (longer texts always have lower TTR)
  - Formula: Root TTR = unique words / square root(total words)
  - Better for comparing texts of different lengths

  Results:
    Brain-Only Root TTR    : 6.13
    GenAI-Assisted Root TTR: 6.04

MEASUREMENT 3: Lexical Density -- Meaningful Words vs Filler Words
  - Counts: how many words are REAL MEANINGFUL words (nouns, verbs, adjectives)
  - vs words that are just connectors (the, a, of, and, in, to, etc.)
  - Higher % = writing is more information-rich and academic

  Results:
    Brain-Only Lexical Density    : 58.4%  (more meaningful content words)
    GenAI-Assisted Lexical Density: 54.1%  (more filler/connector words)

MEASUREMENT 4: Coleman-Liau Readability Index
  - Measures how EASY or HARD the text is to read
  - Based on: average letters per word and average sentences per 100 words
  - Output: a grade level (e.g., Grade 9, Grade 12)
  - Higher grade = more complex academic writing

MEASUREMENT 5: Hapax Legomena Count
  - "Hapax" = Greek for "said only once"
  - Counts words that appear EXACTLY ONCE in the text
  - High hapax count = student used many unique, rarely repeated words

KEY NLP FINDING (statistically significant, p < 0.001):
--------------------------------------------------------
  "Human-only writing achieved significantly higher vocabulary variety.
   Brain-Only writers used more diverse and unique vocabulary (TTR advantage).
   GenAI-Assisted writing was more FLUENT and POLISHED in structure,
   but relied more on standard templates and repeated phrase patterns."

  In simple words:
  --> GenAI helps you write SMOOTHER sentences
  --> But it makes your writing LESS ORIGINAL
  --> And because you think less, you REMEMBER less later

WHAT IS AN API?
----------------
API = Application Programming Interface

Simple explanation:
  The WEBPAGE (HTML/JavaScript) is the FACE of the app -- what you SEE.
  The PYTHON CODE is the BRAIN of the app -- where calculations happen.
  The API is the PHONE LINE between the face and the brain.

When you click "Predict Risk" on the dashboard:
  1. JavaScript (webpage) sends your slider values to Python via the API
  2. Python runs the ML model on those values
  3. Python sends back "HIGH RISK" or "LOW RISK"
  4. JavaScript shows the result on screen
  All in less than 1 second!
